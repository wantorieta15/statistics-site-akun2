TalaStat — an independent presentation of public Philippine statistics. Tala is the Filipino word for “star”.

Data sources: Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) — Census of Population, national accounts, Labour Force Survey and trade statistics; Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) — personal remittances; World Bank — nominal GDP in US dollars. Every figure on this site names its source and year.

Figures are rounded headline values as published; regional populations are the 2024 Census counts. This site works fully offline — no data is fetched from the internet.
